A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

What the numbers mean

Same color. Two coordinate systems.

OKLab uses lightness (L) and opponent axes (a, b). OKLCH describes the same space with lightness, chroma, and hue. Switching tabs converts the coordinates; it doesn’t pick a new color.

Wide gamut is not HDR.

A color outside sRGB may fit Display P3, or it may fit neither. That depends on the color, not a single chroma cutoff. A P3-capable display isn’t a promise of HDR, and lightness above 100% doesn’t unlock extra brightness.

Mapping is an approximation.

Mapped outputs use Culori’s OKLCH gamut mapping. The native preview uses your browser’s color rendering, which can differ from the mapped output. The plane is always an sRGB raster.
